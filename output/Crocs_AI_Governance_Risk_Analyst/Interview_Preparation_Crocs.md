## 5. Deep Technical & Governance Question Bank
1. *Q: How do you design an AI intake and review gate that doesn't bottleneck engineering or product teams?*  
   *Framework:* Implement tiered triage based on use-case risk:
   - *Tier 1 (Low Risk - Internal productivity / draft generation):* Self-service automated checklist with accepted-use guardrails.
   - *Tier 2 (Medium Risk - Internal data analysis, customer-facing assisted workflows):* Standard review covering data sensitivity, model hallucination bounds, vendor terms, and output validation.
   - *Tier 3 (High Risk - Autonomous actions, customer PII/financial processing, SOX-relevant pricing):* Comprehensive pre-launch review gate requiring InfoSec, Legal, and Audit sign-off, strict human-in-the-loop controls, and continuous logging.
2. *Q: How do you maintain an Enterprise AI Risk Register?*  
   *Framework:* Track risk ID, use-case title, business owner, technical architecture, data classification (public vs. confidential vs. PII), key risk dimensions (hallucination, bias, IP leakage, vendor dependency), mitigation controls, testing status, residual risk score, and recurring monitoring frequency.
3. *Q: How do you ensure AI systems remain compliant with SOX or financial audit standards?*  
   *Framework:* Enforce immutable audit trails of all model prompts, inputs, parameters, and outputs; maintain deterministic fallback calculations for financial pricing/discounting; enforce strict role-based access control (RBAC); and require documented human approval before automated ledger updates.
