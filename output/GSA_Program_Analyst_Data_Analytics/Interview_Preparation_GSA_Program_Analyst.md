## 1. Role, Company & Sector Brief

### Agency & Mission
- **Agency:** General Services Administration (GSA) — **Federal Acquisition Service (FAS)**.
- **Mission:** FAS provides buying power to federal agencies, acquiring over $100 billion in products, services, and solutions annually. FAS drives government-wide savings, operational efficiency, and policy compliance across multiple business portfolios, including **GSA Fleet**, Assisted Acquisition Services (AAS), General Supplies and Services (GSS), and Information Technology Category (ITC).
- **GSA Fleet Context:** GSA Fleet manages more than 230,000 non-tactical leased vehicles across the federal government, representing one of the largest civilian fleets in the world. Key operational focus areas include end-to-end vehicle lifecycle management (acquisition, operational telematics, preventative maintenance, fuel utilization, and remarketing/surplus disposal), electrification (Zero-Emission Vehicle / ZEV transition), and vendor/supplier performance.

### Modernization Priorities & Key Analytics Duties
- **Modernization Driver:** FAS is actively executing on the **President's Management Agenda (PMA)** and the **Federal Data Strategy**, transitioning from static, fragmented reporting spreadsheets to integrated analytics, automated data pipelines, and executive business intelligence.
- **Key Duties for GS-0343-13:**
  1. Conduct complex data analysis to support program objectives, streamline supply chain and fleet operations, and enhance operational efficiency.
  2. Lead and execute complex data initiatives and analytical projects to drive FAS business transformation.
  3. Design and build reports, analytical prototypes, and data visualizations tracking vehicle lifecycle milestones, supplier/vendor accountability, customer agency utilization, and multi-million-dollar financial obligations.
  4. Provide technical advice, data governance guidance, and expert analytical recommendations to business lines.
  5. Deliver executive briefings and technical presentations to senior management and program directors regarding BI and data findings.

### Agency Pain Points & Needs
- Disparate legacy data systems across ordering, inventory tracking, leasing accounts, and vendor billing requiring rigorous ETL reconciliation.
- Ensuring high data integrity across supplier performance, procurement schedules, and deobligation of expired funds.
- Translating granular operational data into strategic metrics that federal leaders can use for fleet rightsizing and budget justifications.

## 5. Deep Technical & Strategy Question Bank

### Q1: How do you approach analyzing complex, fragmented datasets to support federal program decisions?
- **Framework & Response:**
  1. **Define Objective & Key Metrics:** Clarify the operational question with program leadership (e.g., Are we experiencing procurement cycle slippage? Are vehicle maintenance costs exceeding baseline thresholds?).
  2. **Data Ingestion & Profiling:** Connect to data sources (SAP financial tables, ordering databases, fleet telematics/billing). Run SQL profiling queries to check completeness, null distributions, duplicate primary keys, and formatting anomalies.
  3. **Data Transformation & Cleansing:** Apply standardized transformation rules—mapping legacy codes into standardized FAS taxonomies, normalizing date formats, and filtering out erroneous transaction runs.
  4. **Exploratory Analysis & Slicing:** Use tools like Tableau or SQL aggregations to evaluate multi-dimensional trends (by region, vehicle class, fiscal year, or vendor).
  5. **Insight Synthesis & Decision Briefing:** Translate findings into executive takeaways (e.g., "30% of repair cost overruns originate from three specific regional vendor facilities due to off-contract labor rates"). Always present findings with actionable recommendations and risk-adjusted options.

### Q2: Walk me through how you would conceptualize and structure a dashboard for vehicle lifecycle metrics.
- **Framework & Response (Keeping to honest exploratory capabilities):**
  1. **Lifecycle Stage Architecture:** Structure the data model across 4 critical milestones: *Procurement/Delivery & Upfitting $\rightarrow$ Operational Utilization & Fuel/Maintenance $\rightarrow$ Mid-Life Reconditioning $\rightarrow$ End-of-Life Remarketing/Disposal*.
  2. **Core KPIs per Dimension:**
     - *Acquisition & Delivery:* Order-to-delivery lead time, manufacturer variance.
     - *Utilization:* Miles per vehicle per month, underutilized asset percentage (identifying candidates for fleet rightsizing or redistribution).
     - *Operating Cost:* Maintenance cost per mile (CPM), fuel efficiency (MPG / kWh equivalent for ZEVs), unscheduled maintenance frequency.
     - *Disposal:* Depreciation rate, resale value recovery ratio against GSA auction benchmarks.
  3. **Visual Hierarchy & Audience Focus:** Design the executive summary layer for senior FAS leaders (high-level fleet health, budget execution, green fleet progress), with intuitive drill-downs for regional fleet managers (specific VIN-level alerts, vendor warranty claims, and overdue scheduled services).
  4. **Data Integrity & Refresh Cadence:** Ensure underlying SQL data extracts link reliably to source systems (e.g., GSA Fleet Drive-thru, financial ledgers) and include clear data-freshness timestamps and discrepancy flags.

### Q3: How have you used data analytics to identify operational inefficiencies and reduce costs?
- **Framework & Response (Anchored in Verified Experience):**
  - **Financial Re-alignment / Deobligation:** At DOI IBC, analyzed multi-year SAP obligation records (`FBL1N`, `ME2M`) against actual vendor IPAC invoice histories. Identified millions of dollars in unliquidated obligations (ULOs) tied to completed or inactive contracts. Designed a systematic reconciliation report that reclaimed over **$1M annually** in recurring funds.
  - **Incident / Ticket Trend Analysis:** Analyzed thousands of Remedy helpdesk incident records. Extracted categories, root causes, and user roles to discover that 40% of repeat tickets were concentrated around specific PRISM invoice approval steps and SAP transaction routing errors. Built targeted cohort training and quick-reference guides for 85+ users, leading to a substantial drop in recurring ticket escalations.

### Q4: How do you handle situations where source data quality is poor, inconsistent, or missing?
- **Framework & Response:**
  1. **Never Conceal Data Issues:** Acknowledge data quality gaps early; never make program assumptions or force calculations on unvalidated figures.
  2. **Root-Cause Triage:** Determine whether the defect is a front-end user entry error (e.g., missing mileage logs), an ETL translation bug (e.g., truncated text fields or misaligned account codes), or an interface timing delay between legacy systems.
  3. **Automated Validation Rules:** Write SQL scripts with assertion checks (e.g., identifying negative mileage values, orphaned purchase orders without corresponding obligations, or future-dated service records).
  4. **Data Remediation & Governance:** Establish clear data dictionary definitions with system owners. Implement cross-system mapping tables (similar to the legacy-to-FBMS crosswalks built at DOI) to resolve discrepancies systematically rather than through manual one-off edits.

### Q5: How would you responsibly apply modern data analytics and AI modernization concepts to GSA FAS programs?
- **Framework & Response:**
  1. **Pragmatic Foundations First:** Modernization starts with solid data hygiene and sound data governance, not deploying complex algorithms on chaotic data. Ensure databases have consistent schema definitions, audit trails, and role-based access.
  2. **Predictive Analytics Opportunities:** In federal fleet and supply management, apply predictive modeling to forecast vehicle component failures, anticipate seasonal supply chain bottlenecks, and optimize auction timing for surplus assets.
  3. **NIST AI Risk Management Framework (RMF):** Apply the 6 key risk dimensions studied at DU—evaluating AI initiatives for privacy, compliance, hallucination, bias, governance, and transparency.
  4. **Human-in-the-Loop Safeguards:** In federal procurement and financial environments, analytical and automated models must serve as decision-support tools for Contracting Officers and Program Managers, maintaining full human accountability for any obligation or disposal decision.

## 7. Verified STAR Story Bank

### Story 1: Remedy IT Ticket Trend Analysis & Operational Improvement
- **Situation:** At the Department of the Interior (DOI IBC) Acquisition Directorate, the support team was overwhelmed by 5 to 10 mission-critical escalations daily from contracting officers and specialists experiencing errors in PRISM and FBMS workflows.
- **Task:** As Senior Escalation Lead, analyze recurring support tickets, identify underlying operational bottlenecks, and implement solutions to reduce ticket volume and improve system adoption.
- **Action:** Extracted and categorized months of Remedy incident ticket data. Executed trend analysis on incident categories, error messages, and user bureaus. Discovered that over 40% of repeat issues stemmed from confusion surrounding invoice approval routing (IPP integration) and contract clause validation. Designed and delivered targeted cohort training sessions for 85+ users and authored clear standard operating procedures (SOPs).
- **Result:** Dramatically cut recurring helpdesk inquiries, accelerated procurement throughput, and improved contracting officer confidence across the directorate.

### Story 2: SAP Financial Reporting & $1M+ Annual Funds Reclaim
- **Situation:** Discrepancies between contracting officials, program offices, and outside vendors left substantial unliquidated obligations (ULOs) sitting dormant across multi-year contracts in DOI's FBMS.
- **Task:** Perform rigorous financial data analysis to track obligations, deobligations, and funds status, identifying dormant dollars eligible for recovery.
- **Action:** Regularly extracted and analyzed detailed SAP financial ledgers using standard and custom transaction codes (`FBL1N` for vendor line items, `ME2M` for purchasing documents by PO, `FAGLL03` for G/L account details, and `ZFI` reports). Built cross-system reconciliation sheets matching PO lines against IPAC billing entries and vendor delivery confirmations. Facilitated structured deobligation workflows between Contracting Officers and Financial Managers.
- **Result:** Successfully reclaimed over **$1 million annually** in recurring contract savings and assisted in recovering millions in previously stranded federal funds, ensuring strict fiscal compliance.

### Story 3: DOI FBMS Department-Wide Data Conversion (ETL Architect)
- **Situation:** The Department of the Interior operated multiple disparate legacy procurement and financial databases across bureaus, resulting in data silos, audit vulnerabilities, and incompatible reporting.
- **Task:** Serve as ETL Architect to plan, execute, and validate the extraction, transformation, and loading of historical procurement and financial records into SAP FBMS.
- **Action:** Conducted comprehensive data profiling across legacy database structures. Developed Oracle SQL and PL/SQL translation rules to sanitize, deduplicate, and convert contract award records, vendor master files, and accounting lines. Partnered with functional leads across Interior bureaus to validate conversion test batches, resolve schema mismatches, and oversee cutover execution.
- **Result:** Successfully migrated the entire Department to a single, unified enterprise system (FBMS). Honored with two major accolades: the **Interior Business Center Award for Excellence** and the **DOI FBMS Special Recognition Award**.

### Story 4: Citibank Identity Scoring Model (Advanced Analytics & Anomaly Detection)
- **Situation:** At Acxiom Corporation, client Citibank was experiencing significant financial losses and compliance risk due to identity theft and fraudulent credit application submissions.
- **Task:** Develop an automated data analytics and identity scoring model (early AI/ML) capable of scoring application risk in real time.
- **Action:** Evaluated massive relational datasets spanning consumer application records, credit bureau feeds, and historical fraud patterns. Engineered statistical scoring algorithms and data transformation rules that verified applicant identity consistency, flagged anomalies, and scored probability of fraud before application approval.
- **Result:** Delivered Citibank’s first Online Identity Scoring Model, preventing millions in fraudulent account losses and later serving as the architectural foundation for large-scale passenger-screening demonstrations to Congress.
